Choose colors, emojis and card styles. Resize and arrange your tiles, organize them into categories, and find a Moment with search and sorting. Put a photo widget or the next event in a category on your Android home screen, with reminders and milestone notifications when you need them.

A story you can replay

With optional Premium, export your photo timeline as a silent MP4 video up to 1080p or a looping GIF up to 720 pixels on its longest side. Choose the total duration and share a sequence that shows how far you have come.

A few things to know

Is Pix-Moments free?

Yes. The free version includes one active Moment of each type (Since, Until and Repeat), with a maximum of three Moments in total, including archived and completed ones. Photos, widgets, reminders, categories and ZIP backup are included. Optional Premium removes the active Moment limit and adds MP4/GIF export.

Does Pix-Moments have ads or subscriptions?

No. Both versions are ad-free. Premium is an optional one-time purchase for lifetime access.

Can I put a countdown or photo on my home screen?

Yes. Long-press an empty area of your Android home screen, open Widgets and choose Pix-Moments. Select a Moment or a category for the Next event widget, then resize it. Steps can vary by launcher.

Are my photos backed up automatically?

No. Your photos and Moments are stored locally. Export a manual ZIP backup from Settings to keep a separate copy or move to another device. There is no automatic cloud synchronization.

Why might a reminder arrive late?

Reminder delivery depends on Android permissions and device settings. Force-stopping the app pauses background work until you open it again.

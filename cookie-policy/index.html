Cookies & your choices

We remember your cookie choices. You can separately allow language storage and Google Analytics for audience measurement.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text values stored by your browser. We use them to remember your choices and, if you agree, to measure use of the website.

Essential cookies: choices and account access

cookie_consent stores your optional-cookie choices for 180 days without a unique visitor identifier. When you use an account, essential cookies maintain your signed-in session for up to 30 days and protect short sign-in, email-validation and security steps. Signing out removes the account session cookie.

Optional cookie: site_language

site_language remembers the language you explicitly select for up to 365 days if you enable language storage.

Google Analytics

With your consent, Google Analytics measures site usage, such as page views. Google receives technical connection and browser data. The _ga and _ga_Y3KP2PKKS3 cookies distinguish browsers and maintain session state for up to 180 days. Pix-kit configures page views without URL query strings or fragments, limits the referring URL to its origin, and disables advertising features in the site tag.

If you refuse or cookies are blocked

Public pages remain available when optional cookies are refused. Account sign-in requires essential cookies. Localized page addresses and tab-session storage can preserve language and cookie choices. Automatic language selection uses your browser settings.

Hosting and external websites

Liquid Web receives connection information to serve and protect this website and host private account data. Google processes Google sign-in and, with consent, Analytics. Google Workspace delivers account emails. Google Play, YouTube and other external websites apply their own policies when you follow their links. App privacy notices cover each Android application.

Change or delete your choices

Use Manage preferences in the footer to change optional language storage and Analytics choices. Disabling a category removes its website cookies. Account session cookies are managed separately by signing in and out. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser; blocking essential cookies may prevent account access.

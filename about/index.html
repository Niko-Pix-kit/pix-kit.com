About Pix-kit

Useful ideas.
Room to play.

We create digital tools and books that make everyday life a little more practical, creative and curious.

Build. Learn. Play.

Pix-kit is a brand for digital content: Android applications, useful tools and books, including stories and learning experiences for children. Different formats, with the same starting point — an idea that helps someone do, understand or imagine something.

Sometimes that means turning a graph into usable numbers. Sometimes it means keeping a meaningful date in sight, or watching a project grow through photos. And sometimes it means giving a young reader something new to discover.

Practical by nature

Tools with a clear purpose.

Our apps start with a concrete task. We focus on making that task approachable, with thoughtful controls and space for people to work in their own way.

Curious at heart

Learning can be playful.

Books and digital experiences can open the same door: a question, an experiment, a story. We make room for discovery, especially for children finding their way through new ideas.

Privacy Policy

How this website uses account, profile and connection information.

Effective date2026-11-07
ProviderNihon Essentials Hub G.K.
Contactinfo@pix-kit.com

1. Website and contact

Nihon Essentials Hub G.K. operates Pix-kit. Liquid Web hosts this website and its private account database and receives connection details such as IP addresses. We use emails you send us to respond. Google Analytics runs only with consent; see the Cookie Policy.

Cookie policy

2. Account access and security

Google sign-in supplies an account identifier, email, name and available profile picture; it does not grant access to Gmail, Drive or contacts. Email accounts use an email address and a salted password hash. Google Workspace delivers validation and recovery emails. Authenticator verification uses an encrypted setup secret and hashed recovery codes. Authenticator verification is optional for members and required for administrators. Your personal security phrase is encrypted and included in account emails. For known accounts, we record sign-in and sign-out attempts, their outcome and time, connection IP address and browser information. The IP address and browser information are encrypted. This history begins when the feature is introduced.

3. Profiles and visibility

We store your nickname, names, country, language and chosen avatar or pixel drawing. Your nickname and site avatar can appear on your public profile. You choose whether names, country and language are public. Your email stays private; your Google profile photo is not public by default. Authorized administrators can privately read account details, profile completion and connection history for security and support. IP addresses and browser information are not public.

4. Storage and deletion

Private account data stays in the hosting database. Essential cookies support account access and security steps. Account connection history is deleted automatically after 30 days. Deleting an account removes its active profile, email identities, security settings, avatar, sessions and connection history. Hosting logs and host-managed backups follow the host’s retention cycle. Private recovery backups created during updates are kept separately with restricted access and require deletion by the site operator; contact us about data remaining in backups. Providers may process information outside Japan.

5. Your choices

Edit your profile, visibility and security settings in My account, or delete your account there. Contact info@pix-kit.com for privacy questions or requests concerning your information. Android applications have separate privacy policies.

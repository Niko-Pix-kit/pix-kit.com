Pix-Moments Privacy Policy

Local moments and photos, manual backups and optional Google Play purchases.

Summary

Pix-Moments stores your moments and photos on your Android device. It does not require an account for local use. Google Drive connection and synchronization have been removed. There is no advertising, analytics, behavioural tracking or remote crash-reporting service. Pix-Kit does not receive or sell your moments or photos.

Local information and photos

The app stores titles, dates, descriptions, types, categories, appearance, card layouts, milestones, reminders, archive status and photo series in private Android storage. Local identifiers, edit revisions, deleted-record markers, widget associations and notification delivery records support editing and prevent duplicate actions. They are not advertising identifiers. Theme, language and other app preferences are also stored locally.

Photo capture requires contextual camera permission. Cover selection uses Android's photo picker, without broad photo-library access. App-owned copies are saved privately; original gallery photos are not changed or deleted. Photo alignment and camera guidance run on the device. Captured originals and aligned copies can be included in manual backups.

Android system cloud backup and automatic device-transfer backup are disabled. The local database and ZIP exports do not have a separate encryption password. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local information; export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.

Manual backup and video export

Settings can export or import a ZIP containing moments, descriptions, photos, milestones and supported preferences. The empty home screen also offers backup import. Account information, purchase receipts, review reminders, widget host IDs and notification delivery records are excluded. Imports are validated and preserve existing records, creating copies when identifiers conflict.

Premium export creates a silent MP4 locally, using capture timestamps for photo durations. Temporary export files remain in private cache until you choose a destination. They are cleaned after completion or cancellation; abandoned files become eligible for cleanup on the next export after 24 hours. Saved exports remain under your control.

You choose ZIP and MP4 destinations using Android's document picker. A selected document provider may upload a file under its own terms. Pix-Moments itself does not automatically synchronize your data. Exported files are not password protected and must be deleted separately from app data.

Widgets, reminders and deletion

Widgets and notifications can expose your content to anyone who can see your home or lock screen, depending on Android settings. Notifications are optional and require permission where applicable. Android may delay background work.

Deleting a moment removes its record and reconciles widgets and notifications. Archiving retains the moment and pauses its reminders. Local deleted-record markers prevent stale edits from restoring a deleted identifier. Unreferenced app-owned photo copies become eligible for cleanup after seven days; original gallery photos are unaffected. Notification delivery records are pruned after seven days during maintenance.

Upgrading from the Drive version

The update stops this installation's old synchronization jobs and clears its saved Drive account preferences. Existing local moments, photos and other settings remain. It does not contact Drive to retrieve or delete previous cloud copies. Other devices running older versions can still have their own copies or active synchronization.

You can manage old authorization through your Google account connections and old application data through Google Drive's app management. Removing this application or upgrading it does not delete those previous cloud copies.

Premium and review reminders

Google Play processes the optional one-time Premium purchase. Pix-Moments receives the product identifier, purchase status, token and signed receipt used to verify and restore access. A receipt is retained privately for offline access. Google Play handles payment card details; Pix-Kit does not receive them. Purchase records are excluded from backups. Uninstalling clears the local receipt; access can be restored with the purchasing Google Play account. Google processes billing under its own terms and privacy policy.

Review reminders store first-use time, active-day counts, reminder dates and whether reminders were stopped. These settings remain local and are not exported. Choosing to rate opens Google Play. Pix-Moments cannot read whether you submitted a review or its rating. Automatic invitations stop when you choose to rate or say you already reviewed.

Security, contact and changes

Private Android storage, validated inputs and transactional writes help protect app data. No system guarantees complete security. Opening the public policy uses your browser and the website host's services; it does not upload your moments. If you contact support, we receive only information you choose to send.

Pix-Moments is a general date-tracking utility. Pix-Kit does not knowingly collect children's moment data on its servers. This policy is updated when data practices change. Store disclosures must reflect the versions being distributed.

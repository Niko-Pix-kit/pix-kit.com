Summary

Pix-Algebra does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal information. The app is designed to work locally on the device without user accounts, advertising, analytics, cloud sync, or third-party tracking.

1. Information handled by the app

Pix-Algebra may save learning progress locally on the device, such as XP, streaks, completed exercises, correct answers, timing totals, selected settings, and preferred learning methods.

This information stays on the user's device. It is not sent to Pix-kit or to third parties.

2. Data collection

Pix-Algebra does not collect personal information from users.

  • No account is required.
  • No name, email address, phone number, location, photos, contacts, or files are collected.
  • No advertising identifier is collected.
  • No analytics SDK is used.
  • No data is transmitted to Pix-kit servers.

3. Data sharing

Pix-Algebra does not share user data with advertisers, analytics providers, cloud providers, or other third parties.

4. Children’s privacy

Pix-Algebra is designed as an educational tool that may be used by children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information related to Pix-Algebra, contact us so we can help address the issue.

5. Advertising and purchases

Pix-Algebra does not display third-party advertising and does not include in-app purchases.

6. Permissions

Pix-Algebra does not require sensitive Android permissions for its core functionality.

7. Local data deletion

Users can remove local app data by uninstalling Pix-Algebra or by clearing the app's storage from Android system settings. Local progress may also be reset from inside the app when that feature is available.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Pix-Algebra changes. When changes are made, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.
